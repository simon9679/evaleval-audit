# POSTRUN_METHOD_NOTE — T09

Status: post-run meta-audit note.

## Method behavior observed

T09 carried an internal counterfactual result to a product-facing aggregate and
measured its exact corpus magnitude.

The result is neither zero nor large.

That distinction is important.

A local finding can reach a product surface while having a small aggregate
effect. A falsification-oriented audit should report both facts.

## Protocol contribution

Rule 7 was decisive: internal arithmetic evidence was not treated as a product
finding until the product-facing aggregate was recomputed.

Rule 8 kept the product counterfactual fixed.

The cheapest-to-more-expensive ordering now supports semantic adjudication only
for two product-consequential groups.

## Handbook contribution

U8 constrained the result to product-aggregate exposure rather than semantic
invalidity.

U9 preserved the dependency chain.

U14 required an effect-resolving product measurement.

B12 kept comparability eligibility as the governing decision boundary.

## Candidate methodological lesson

When an internal measurement defect candidate reaches a public aggregate,
report both:

1. whether the aggregate changes;
2. the absolute and relative magnitude of that change.

Do not use exposure alone as a proxy for materiality.
